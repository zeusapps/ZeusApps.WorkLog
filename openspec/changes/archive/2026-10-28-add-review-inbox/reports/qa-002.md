## Assessment and limitations

All three rechecked IDs pass on the A5 target. Regression is green (build, 263 .NET tests, 81 Angular tests, 19 of 19 packaged e2e). The overview follows a marker removal within about 3 s, consistent with the catalog's 2 s cache plus the poll. Read-store bounds hold.

Limits and decisions:
- The marker-cache latency figure (0.53 ms at 900 documents) was not independently re-measured; the .NET suite covers the bound as a unit test only.
- In a foreign-schema index, `get` and `summary` are silent (no baselines) and only `mark` returns 503. This matches the store's documented contract and the client shows no notice, so I treat it as acceptable, not a finding.
- The idle window was 150 s (the probe labelled it 135 s internally; the count of 16 and 8 reflects two rounds either way).
- Disposable data: all `artifacts/qa-002-*` directories (including the package) were removed. No probe is running; ports 47981 to 47986 are released. Probe scripts remain in the session scratchpad only.
- Writes: this report and the screenshots `../screenshots/qa-002-reads-locked.png` and `../screenshots/qa-002-reads-recovered.png`.
